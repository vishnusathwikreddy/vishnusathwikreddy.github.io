నమస్తే, నేను Sathwik Reddy.

I'm an undergraduate student and researcher at IIIT Hyderabad, working with Prof. Ponnurangam Kumaraguru (PK) in the Precog research group.

My research interests lie in neural network architectures and learning algorithms. I'm fascinated by the question of whether we're approaching intelligence with the right computational structures and learning paradigms. I enjoy exploring unconventional ideas that challenge established assumptions about how neural networks are built, trained, and scaled.

Rather than restricting myself to incremental improvements, I'm particularly drawn to fundamentally different approaches to building intelligent systems. (Thanks to Arjit Jain for encouraging me to question the assumptions behind conventional approaches and to think radically differently about research problems.)

I've had the opportunity to work closely with Prof. Chirag Agarwal (University of Virginia), from whom I've learned—and continue to learn—a great deal about research. I've had the good fortune of working with Prof. Jisun An (Indiana University) and Dr. Prashant Kodali (Microsoft Research).

If you're interested in similar (or related) problems, I'm happy to connect!
