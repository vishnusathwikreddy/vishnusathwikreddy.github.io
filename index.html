నమస్తే, నేను Sathwik Reddy。

I'm an undergraduate student (and) researcher at IIIT Hyderabad, working with the Precog research group under Prof. Ponnurangam Kumaraguru (PK).

I'm currently investigating reasoning in continuous latent spaces. More broadly, I'm interested in making models more intelligent, robust, and efficient. If you're interested in similar (or related) problems, I'm happy to connect!

I've had the opportunity to work closely with Prof. Chirag Agarwal (University of Virginia), from whom I've learned—and continue to learn—a great deal about research and how to think about problems. I'm currently also working with Arjit Jain (TurboML), who has taught me to question the assumptions behind conventional approaches and to think radically differently about research problems. I've had the good fortune of working with Prof. Jisun An (Indiana University).
